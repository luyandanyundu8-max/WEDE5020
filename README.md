## About the Project
Cake Heaven is a small, home‑based bakery that started on **20 May 2022**.  
It was born from a grandmother’s secret recipes, and we now bring that same love for baking to you online.  
This website is our digital shop – it lets you browse, customise, and order our homemade cakes from anywhere.

### What We Want to Achieve
- **Be easy to find** – search engines will help new customers discover us.  
- **Be simple to use** – whether on a phone or computer, ordering should take just a few clicks.  
- **Show our cakes beautifully** – clear photos and descriptions help you choose.  
- **Connect with you** – through testimonials, contact forms, and social media.  
- **Grow our business** – we track what’s popular so we can keep getting better.

---

## Key Features
### Pages on the Website
- **Home** – welcome message, our story, featured cakes, seasonal specials.  
- **Products** – cakes sorted by occasion (birthday, wedding, cupcakes).  
- **Order** – pick a size, choose a flavour, tell us where to deliver.  
- **About Us** – who we are and what makes our cakes special.  
- **Contact** – form for questions, custom designs, or feedback.

### Technical Highlights
- Built with **WordPress** (easy to update).  
- **SSL secure** for safe payments.  
- Works on **all devices** (phones, tablets, desktops).  
- Connected to **social media** (Instagram, Facebook).  
- **Analytics** to see which cakes are most loved.
